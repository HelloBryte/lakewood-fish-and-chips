## 2. Trading details — blocking

- **Postcode** for the shop address (we've assumed Frankston 3199).
- **Realistic prep time.** Currently set to 20 minutes. This is the number customers judge
  you on. Is it different on a Friday night?
- **Last orders** — currently assumed 15 minutes before close.
- **Public holidays** — closed, or different hours?

## 3. How orders reach the kitchen — blocking for a real launch

The demo doesn't send anything anywhere. For a working shop the order has to physically
arrive. Options, cheapest first:

- Email to a shop address, read off a phone or tablet
- SMS alert (small per-message cost)
- A tablet on the counter showing a live order list
- A thermal docket printer

Also needed: **who cancels an order and refunds it** when something's sold out.

## 4. Payments — decision needed for a real launch

The demo has a fake card form that processes nothing. Taking real prepaid orders needs:

- ABN and registered trading name
- A Stripe (or similar) account and the shop's bank details
- Roughly 1.7% + 30c per transaction in fees on Australian cards

Worth deciding: **prepaid, or pay at pickup?** Prepaid kills no-shows but means handling
refunds. Pay-at-pickup needs no payment setup at all and could go live immediately.

## 5. Content that would lift this a lot — not blocking

- **Photos.** The single biggest improvement available. Six to ten daylight phone shots:
  the shop front, an open parcel of fish and chips, the burger, a souvlaki, a family pack.
  Right now the site has no food photography at all.
- **The logo as an image file** — the shark artwork, not a photo of the printed menu. The
  current fish on the site is a stand-in we drew.
- **Google Business Profile link and review rating.** The neighbouring shop leads with its
  star rating; it converts.
- **Two or three sentences about the shop** in the owner's own words. The current copy is
  deliberately neutral because we didn't want to invent history.
- **Allergen statement.** Shared fryer, gluten, nut oils. Not optional in Australia once
  you're taking orders.
- **Domain name**, if the shop wants one.
